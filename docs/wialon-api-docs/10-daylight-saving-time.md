# Daylight saving time

The following flags can be used to set daylight saving time:

| Flag | Value |
| --- | --- |
| None | 0x08000 000 |
| 0x0A050 | Russia: time zones before October 26, 2014 000 |
| 0x0B030       Europe: from the last Sunday of March to the last Sunday | 000           of October |
| 0x0A010       United States, Canada: from the second Sunday of March | 000           to the first Sunday of November |
| 0x0A040       Jordan: from the last Friday of February to the last Friday | 000           of October |
| 0x0A060 | Syria: time zones before October 4, 2022 000 |
| 0x0A070   Turks and Caicos Islands: from the first Sunday of April to | 000       the last Sunday of October |
| 0x0A080   Cuba: from the second Sunday of March to the first | 000       Sunday of November |
| 0x0A0C0   Iran: from March 22 to September 22 (from March 21 to | 000       September 21 in a leap year) |
| 0x0A0D0   Israel: From the Friday before the last Sunday of March to | 000       the last Sunday of October |
| 0x0A210   Namibia: from the first Sunday of September to the first | 000       Sunday of April |
| 0x0A220   New Zealand: from the last Sunday of September to the | 000       last Sunday of April |
| 0x0A230   Chile: from the first Sunday of September to the first | 000       Sunday of April |
| 0x0A240   Brazil: from the third Sunday of October to the third | 000       Sunday of February |
| 0x0A250   Paraguay: from the first Sunday of October to the last | 000       Sunday of March |
| 0x0A270   Australia: from the first Sunday of October to the first | 000       Sunday of April |
| 0x0A280   Tasmania: from the first Sunday of October to the first | 000       Sunday of April |
| 0x0A290   Fiji: from the first Sunday of November to the third | 000       Sunday of January |
